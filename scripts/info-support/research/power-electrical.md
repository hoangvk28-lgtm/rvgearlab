MPPT vs PWM: PWM panel voltage should match battery; controller amps = array W / battery V x 1.25. MPPT must handle cold Voc (Voc rises as temp drops; use panel temp coefficient and record low; flat NEC 690.7 x1.25 shortcut for temperate US climates). MPPT gain cited ~15-30 percent, bigger in cold. https://www.thegreenwatt.com/solar-charge-controller-size-calculator/ ; https://portlandiaelectric.supply/blogs/inverter/solar-charge-controller-sizing-selector ; https://www.renogy.com/blog/solar-charge-controller-sizing-and-how-to-choose-one-/
EMS thresholds: https://www.rvtravel.com/rv-electricity-surge-protector-types/ (disconnect above ~128 V, below ~104 V)
